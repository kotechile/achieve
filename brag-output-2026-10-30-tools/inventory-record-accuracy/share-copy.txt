65% of your stock records are wrong. That is $11.1m of buffer.

DESCRIPTION
Safety stock gets sized against demand and lead time, almost never against the error in the records the system reads. At 65% record variance that error adds 184 units per SKU: $11.1m of working capital, $2.4m a year to hold, and $5.4m of margin lost where the shelf is empty and the ERP says it is not.
