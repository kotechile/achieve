## Thumbnail

`youtube-thumbnail.jpg` — 1280x720, uploaded as the custom thumbnail (YouTube requires
1280x720 and under 2 MB; this file is both).

It is not a still from the video. At 1280x720 in a feed, a frame from a 1920x1080 film
reads as small type on a dark field — the numbers vanish at the 210px-wide size YouTube
actually renders a thumbnail at. This thumbnail is built for that size:

- `$681,000` set at 168px on the left, `$12,380` at 76px on the right — the whole
  argument legible at phone width
- the amber bar at 100% against the steel bar at 1.8%, the same proportional language the
  video and the tool's ledger use
- `91% OFF THE INVOICE` in the lower band as the hook for anyone who reads the small tier
- same palette, fonts and hairlines as the film and the product, so the click and the
  watch are the same object

Do not use `brag.jpg` as the YouTube thumbnail — it is 1920x1080 and its type is sized for
a full-screen frame. It exists to be the video's frame 0 and the poster for any embedded
player.
